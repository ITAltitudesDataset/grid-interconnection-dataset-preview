## 8. Known Limitations

- Interconnection project status may change after the reporting period.
- Projects may be withdrawn, modified, suspended, or completed.
- Queue inclusion does not guarantee project construction.
- Historical records may contain missing or inconsistent information.
- Information may differ between grid operators and reporting periods.
- The dataset is not a live operational grid monitoring service.
